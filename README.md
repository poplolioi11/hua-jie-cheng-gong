# 化劫成功論（DRS 428685230） / Hua jie cheng gong lun: Transcription Dataset

底本為哈佛燕京圖書館藏本 DRS 428685230，索書號 RAREB TA 1980.2 2451。內題葉並列光緒辛卯十七年與西曆一千八百九十一年，刊記為青州府救世會藏板。 [館目](https://id.lib.harvard.edu/alma/990094311760203941/catalog) · [固定掃描](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:Harvard_drs_428685230_%E5%8C%96%E5%8A%AB%E6%88%90%E5%8A%9F%E8%AB%96.pdf&oldid=1293876313) · [館藏 IIIF](https://iiif.lib.harvard.edu/manifests/view/drs:428685230)

《化劫成功論》是青州府救世會 1891 年刊印的華文基督教小冊。題葉並列光緒辛卯十七年與西曆紀年。鄭雨人序稱原作者為秀耀春（Francis Huberty James），自序署秀華陽。正文從傳道遭逼迫的由來切入，兼論聽道者的反感、教友言行不一，以及困厄如何轉成傳道者彼此勉勵的契機。篇末以今世及後世的平安和蒙賜收束。可供晚清山東傳教出版及教會對地方衝突的論述研究。

本資料集以哈佛燕京藏本 DRS 428685230 為底本，共取 32 幅固定影像，其中 20 幅用於轉錄正文，提供 JSON、TXT、TEI 和逐項覆核紀錄。此版為 AI 校讀稿，312 項比對中保留 39 項未解。人工全文二校待完成。

Hua jie cheng gong lun is a Chinese Christian pamphlet issued in 1891 by the Jiushihui of Qingzhou. Its title leaf gives the Guangxu and Western dates. Zheng Yuren’s preface names the author as Xiu Yaochun (Francis Huberty James), whose own preface is signed Xiu Huayang. The body considers why preaching meets persecution, discussing hostile reception, inconsistencies between what believers say and do, and how hardship can become a reason to encourage others. It closes with assurances of peace and blessing in this world and the next. The pamphlet supports research on late Qing missionary printing and responses to local conflict in Shandong.

This dataset uses 32 fixed images from Harvard-Yenching Library DRS 428685230. The main text is transcribed from 20 pages. The AI-reviewed JSON, TXT and TEI transcription retains 39 unresolved comparison items. Full human collation is outstanding.

書中首序稱英國教士秀耀春，自序署秀華陽。大英浸信會 1891 年年報印頁 56 記 Francis James 從濟南報告，他應 Whitewright 請託寫成 *Persecution, its Causes, Remedies, and Effects*，題名與底本前封後加的英文題記相合。香港漢語基督教文化研究所的人名索引將「秀耀春」對應為 Francis Huberty James。 [同時代年報](https://missiology.org.uk/pdf/annual-report-of-the-bms/annual-report-of-the-bms_1891.pdf#page=77) · [人名索引](https://iscs.org.hk/Template/Shared/Books/PeviewFile/LPT15/appendix2b.pdf)

## 資料與校讀

- data/document.json 保留歷史文字、原圖位置、疑讀與缺口。
- data/text.txt 與 data/text.tei.xml 為全文與 TEI，附文字單位的對應檔。
- data/review.json 保留 312 項比對、39 項未解及採用紀錄。
- data/manifest.json 與 PACKAGE-SHA256SUMS.txt 記錄固定發行的檔案雜湊。

底本 32 幅。前封題簽與題葉各記位置，p003–p010 為兩篇序，p011–p030 為正文。p031–p032 的透印、後加館記及底面按影像記錄。

The 32 fixed images include the front cover, title leaf, two prefaces in p003–p010, and the body in p011–p030. The last two image records describe show-through, later library marks, and the rear cover as visible.

初錄與第二路讀稿均以固定原圖為據。Claude 完成初錄第 1–30 幅與第二路讀稿第 1–28 幅。Codex 補齊其餘幅次，並據原圖合校。程式列出的 312 項比對均有逐項紀錄。採入 17 項有影像依據的更正，保留未解位置與候選。另有 1 項異讀保留原讀及候選。程式驗收確認公開 JSON、TXT 和 TEI 的逐欄內容一致。TEI 通過 P5 4.12.0 Relax NG。人工全文二校、專家審定和準確率量測待完成。

Both readings used the fixed source images. Claude produced the first reading for images 1–30 and the second reading for images 1–28. Codex completed the remaining images and collated the readings against the source. The dataset records 312 comparison items. The edition incorporates 17 corrections supported by the images. The initial readings and alternatives remain recorded for 1 further proposed changes. Automated validation confirmed that the transcription content agrees column by column across JSON, TXT and TEI. The TEI passed P5 4.12.0 Relax NG validation. Full human collation, expert assessment and accuracy measurement are outstanding.
